# Keys To Successful Co-Founder Relationships

[Watch Video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A4SLDQDXdp0)

## The Value of a Co-Founder
* Dramatically increases productivity, accountability, and moral support. 
* Solo founders can succeed, but they must prove they can make significant progress alone.

## Finding & Evaluating
* Look within your network first, or use the YC Co-Founder Matching Platform (fill out your profile completely, highlight your traction/MVP, and meet matches within two weeks).
* Treat the relationship like a marriage: align early on goals, stress responses, communication styles, financial runway, and time commitments.
* Non-technical founders should find a technical co-founder rather than hiring a dev shop, which is expensive and slow to iterate.
* Execute a structured 2-4 week trial project with a tangible goal before fully committing.

## Splitting Equity
* Default to an equal equity split to ensure equal motivation over a 7-10 year journey.
* Ideas are cheap; the execution is what holds value. Do not split unequally based on who had the idea, age, or early progress.

## Trust & Communication
* Trust people by default to avoid micromanagement. 
* Create psychological safety for mistakes. Learn together instead of assigning blame or saying "I told you so."
* Spend physical time together to lower communication friction.

## Decision Making & Habits
* Do not make decisions by consensus. Define clear areas of ownership and name a single CEO to act as the ultimate tiebreaker.
* Understand how your co-founder reacts under stress (e.g., attacking vs. withdrawing).
* Hold regularly scheduled 1-on-1s dedicated to bi-directional positive and constructive feedback.
* Do not delay hard conversations; address problems while they are small.
* Disagree and commit: argue fully, but once a decision is made by the owner, support it completely.
