# Setting KPIs and Goals

[Video Link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6DTK9yDP6p0)

## Core Philosophy
* Setting the right Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and strictly prioritizing tasks is essential for early-stage startups to accelerate their path to product-market fit.
* Focus your time only on the work that will have the highest direct impact on your primary goals.

## Choosing the Right KPIs
* **Prioritize Revenue Growth:** For most startups, revenue growth is the ultimate measure of traction and should usually be the primary KPI.
* **Avoid Vanity Metrics:** Metrics like raw user signups can be highly misleading if they are not tied to actual revenue or deep engagement.
* **Charge Customers Early:** Start charging users as early as possible. Getting feedback from paying customers provides a much more accurate signal of value than feedback from free users.

## 5-Step Prioritization Framework
1. **Identify Top KPIs & Goals:** Determine your primary KPIs and establish concrete weekly goals to track your progress.
2. **Find the Bottleneck:** Analyze your startup to identify the single biggest bottleneck that is currently restricting the growth of those KPIs.
3. **Rank Potential Tasks:** Evaluate your list of potential tasks by assessing their likelihood of success against their level of complexity.
4. **Select High-Impact Work:** Pick and execute only the high-impact tasks that will directly address your biggest bottleneck and improve the top KPIs.
5. **Adjust Quickly:** Monitor your metrics closely and pivot or adjust your task prioritization rapidly if the KPIs are not improving.

## Common Mental Traps to Avoid
* **Perfectionism:** Delaying launches or updates in the pursuit of a flawless product.
* **Busywork:** Spending time on vanity tasks that feel productive but do not drive real, measurable progress toward your core metrics.
* **Misallocated Focus:** Choosing to fix small, easy issues instead of tackling the large, difficult problems that actually matter.

## Actionable Steps
* Write down your specific KPIs explicitly.
* Set concrete targets using both Top-Down and Bottoms-Up planning.
* Review your daily task list to ensure every single item aligns with hitting your KPI targets.
* Share your goals with others to build accountability.
* Work strictly on tasks that help achieve product-market fit faster.
